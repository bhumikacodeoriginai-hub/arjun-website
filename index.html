FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Arjun Realty operate in?

We operate across India and the UAE, with our headquarters in Dubai. We serve clients in both markets with world-class warehousing infrastructure.

What is the minimum warehouse size you develop?

We develop warehousing facilities from 10,000 sqft to over 2,00,000 sqft depending on client requirements. Each project is tailored to specific operational needs.

How long does a typical warehouse project take?

Depending on size and complexity, projects typically range from 6 to 18 months from concept to completion, including all regulatory approvals.

Do you offer Build-to-Suit solutions?

Yes, all our warehousing facilities are custom-designed and built to suit each client's specific operational requirements, ensuring maximum efficiency.

What certifications do your facilities have?

Our facilities comply with ISO 9001:2015, LEED green building standards, and all local safety regulations in both India and UAE.
